Let There Be Light
Tyndale’s Words, 500 Years On
Eight familiar phrases, each shown two ways: before Tyndale — the Latin Vulgate and John Wycliffe’s 1395 English, translated from Latin, not the original tongues — and after, in Tyndale’s own 1520s–30s translation, much of it still read today.
1 · Let there be light — Gen. 1:3
5 · A stranger in a strange land — Ex. 2:22
2 · Am I my brother’s keeper? — Gen. 4:9
6 · Let my people go — Ex. 5:1
3 · In the beginning was the Word — John 1:1
7 · To heal the brokenhearted — Luke 4:18
4 · The powers that be — Rom. 13:1–2
8 · He gave up the ghost — Matt. 27:50
